Little Hollow

Seven letters, one very small planet, no rush. Collect each letter at the Post Office and carry it to a neighbour before the lamps burn low.

Move with W A S D or the arrow keys. On touch screens, drag anywhere. Q and E turn the view. Keys 1, 2 and 3 send reactions.

Inspired by Messenger from abeto. The world, art and code here are original.
